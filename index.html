About Me

I am a Junior Data Analyst based in Pretoria, South Africa, with hands-on experience using SQL, Power BI, and Excel to transform raw data into actionable business insights.

My workflow spans the full analytics process — from data cleaning and preparation to structured querying in Databricks SQL, and finally communicating findings through interactive Power BI dashboards.

I am open to Junior Data Analyst opportunities in South Africa and remote roles worldwide.
